EPA bathing-water operations: `epa_bathing_locations`, `epa_bathing_alerts`, and `epa_bathing_measurements` (season: `all`, `in-season`, `out-season`). Alerts and measurements accept `page` and `limit` (maximum 50). `epa_bathing_locations` reads the whole register (about 240 sites, cached for an hour) and accepts `name` and `county` filters (accent-insensitive) plus `offset`/`limit`; follow `next_offset` to page without gaps. All preserve relevant dates and return evidence metadata. Pages are not promised to contain the latest samples; annual classifications and absent alerts do not establish current swimming safety. Locations use Irish Grid eastings/northings, not WGS84 coordinates. Only documented pagination and season routes are used upstream; name/county filtering of locations happens on this server, and there is no date filtering. Alerts and measurements use a five-minute cache; report any stale response.
